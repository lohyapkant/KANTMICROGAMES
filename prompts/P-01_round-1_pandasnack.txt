PART 2. DESIGN COMMITMENTS FOR THIS GAME

* Stage: 100 x 100 design pixels; pastel two-tone checker meadow with dot-flowers,
  cached to an offscreen canvas and blitted each frame; whole-number pixel scale only.
* Panda: composed from doughy blobs (head, ears, body, feet, blush, nose), always
  looking at the player; walks with a 1-pixel lean and waddling feet; blinks at random;
  squashes behind a happy mouth when it eats one shoot; droops and rolls one tear at
  game over. Dash adds breeze lines drawn behind the panda.
* Wasps: 9 x 7 string maps with a two-frame wing flap and a gentle sine bob; they
  enter at a random side row, keep away from the panda's row at spawn, and drift across;
  the difficulty table releases faster bands as the score grows.
* Bamboo: 7 x 9 string map, two sway frames; two shoots always on the meadow, never
  spawning on the panda or on each other; munching bursts six gold sparkles.
* HUD: three pixel hearts top-left inside the stage (shift to empty on loss); a
  rounded dash gauge with a tiny leaf that refills through the dash cooldown.
* Text in the stage: the shared 3 x 5 pixel font (A-Z, 0-9, some punctuation), never a
  browser-smooth font; optional one-pixel shadow pass for readability.
* Controls: WASD plus arrows on desktop, four pads plus a DASH pad plus RESTART on
  phones (pointer events, setPointerCapture, touch-action none); RESTART and the R key
  restart instantly from any state; pressing any key or tapping the stage starts from
  the title and the game-over screen; tapping the stage during play dashes.
* No audio (per the plan's "not doing now"); runs are endless and end only on zero hearts.
